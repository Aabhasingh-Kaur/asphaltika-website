About ASPHALTIKA

Operating across industrial supply, infrastructure materials, and consultancy support since 2019.

ASPHALTIKA is an industrial and infrastructure materials supplier founded in 2019 by Nivesh Jain. The company supports businesses that need dependable sourcing for bitumen products, transformer oil, industrial insulation oil, Light Diesel Oil, and related infrastructure materials.

Alongside material supply, ASPHALTIKA provides consultancy support for BIS, ISO, NABL, and road construction requirements. This combination helps customers manage both practical procurement needs and documentation-oriented project requirements with a single responsive point of contact.

With pan-India operations and a clear industrial focus, ASPHALTIKA works with contractors, manufacturers, infrastructure businesses, and industrial buyers who value reliable communication, practical coordination, and professional support.
