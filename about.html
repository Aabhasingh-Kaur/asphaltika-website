About the company

Supplying industrial and infrastructure businesses with dependable materials since 2019.

ASPHALTIKA was founded by Nivesh Jain to serve the practical material needs of road, infrastructure, manufacturing, and industrial customers across India.

Our focus

Professional supply support for buyers who need clarity, consistency, and industry understanding.

ASPHALTIKA provides bitumen products, transformer oil, Light Diesel Oil, insulation oil, and consultancy services for compliance and road construction work.

The company combines material supply coordination with practical consultancy support, helping customers source key products and move projects forward with confidence.

Our Vision

ASPHALTIKA aims to become a one-stop solution provider for infrastructure and industrial requirements across products and consultancy services.

We are dedicated to building a grounded, scalable, and trusted B2B ecosystem that meets the exact needs of modern industrial operations.
